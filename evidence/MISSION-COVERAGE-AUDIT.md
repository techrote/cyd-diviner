This audit distinguishes delivered measurement coverage from unavailable fixtures and the owner's later expanded requests. **The expanded mission is not complete.** New packed-colour, PCM and proprietary continuous SDR results must not be inferred from successful builds, host tests or the existing packet-echo captures.

baseline/archive coverage table below retains its original scope. SDR is no

are separate outcomes still requiring their own evidence. Neither earlier raw

The outstanding **measurement** gaps are specific: physical touchscreen contact-to-photon timing, analog DAC/loopback continuity and latency, independently calibrated temperature, physical C3 execution, a total profiler-on/off comparison against ordinary or minimally counted firmware, matched-job wireless offload, and independent radiated-power/spectral/cessation verification. These should stay NOT RUN or unknown; successful host tests cannot fill them. The new SDR calibration does supply actual received-envelope evidence, with the documented passband and sample-gap limits.

The existing histogram A/B is useful but narrower than total observer overhead. `perf enable 0` retains timestamps/hooks, frame/audio counters, tick observation, memory, sample infrastructure and ordinary task costs; it disables histogram insertion. The menu's capped frame rate can conceal a small difference. Both baseline and supplementary A/B results are real measurements of that insertion toggle, not a measurement that the full profiler is free. Static sample/histogram arrays alone occupy 24,008 bytes; other observer allocations also remain.

The owner's expanded colour request has a built, host-verified implementation and 36 planned windows, but no physical results in the two earlier closed archives. It uses genuine packed RGB111/RGB444/RGBA5551 eight-row intermediates around the existing RGB565 raster path, with unchanged RGB565 transfer and backing reservation. Alpha coverage derives from changed pixels after the existing blend; it is not native one-bit-alpha raster/history. Results must retain `color_run` receipts proving which layout was actually applied. Likewise the PCM MVP is a new synthetic digital packet-stream experiment; optional packetized PDM does not fulfill the separately clarified proprietary continuous SDR arm. Neither proves physical analog latency. The separately authorized SDR calibration is now closed, as described in the addendum above; its new results are outside those earlier archives.

The pinned classic SDR TX SDK, ESP-IDF `b774170ff46c393eeb5e495ea37936038d3f4f4f` (v5.5.5), also exports `temprature_sens_read` from `components/esp_phy/lib/esp32/librtc.a`. Symbol presence proves availability, not a documented calibrated temperature contract or identity with a ROM implementation. The calibration's before/after raw readings occur outside symbol timing and retain the undocumented/uncalibrated caveat. A software-requested RF deadline and a void tone-off call do not independently prove a hard RF emission bound or measured RF cessation.

The audit found three historical wording problems and corrected their scope without changing measurements: [the guide](../docs/PERFORMANCE.md) and [baseline findings](BASELINE-FINDINGS.md) now link the completed S3/wireless supplements; the recommendations' worksheet heading now identifies **matched-job** transfer terms as unmeasured. The earlier USB truncation, generic BLE error and failed allocation remain intact. The two audio documents, [firmware method](../tools/perf/AUDIO_MVP.md) and [host evidence tools](../tools/perf/AUDIO-MVP.md), describe different parts of the same experiment; their similar names merit consolidation or an explicit cross-link before final delivery.

Two derived-index caveats remain review follow-ups, not corrections to raw captures. Terminal FAILED captures retain a final `IN PROGRESS` row from the interrupted operation; those rows are incomplete and must never enter measured aggregates. Wireless rows in `measurement-status.csv` have blank flat source/image fields because their identities are recorded per endpoint in the underlying dual-board capture. Consumers must follow those endpoint identities; a future flat-index extension should export both endpoints rather than invent one shared firmware hash. The timestamped `mission-status.json` is a snapshot, not a live assertion that newly built images were already tested.
